dash_go hosts fast web tools alongside the CIM EDGE Dash application. Every page shares the same header, light and dark themes, English and Chinese interface, and sign-in.

Signing in and signing out

Protected module pages require your Dash account. You can sign in in three ways (depending on what is enabled on this server):

  1. From the Dash application: click the module button inside Dash. It signs you in automatically with a one-time link.
  2. Account and password: open Sign in, enter your employee ID, name or e-mail address together with your password, and press Sign in. The employee ID is the least ambiguous.
  3. Windows (SSO): press Sign in with Windows (SSO) on the login page. A small window opens on your company computer and closes by itself (allow pop-ups for this site).

Note: Signing out at the top right ends your session in dash_go only; it does not sign you out of the Python Dash application.

Page guide (tour)

The page guide dims the screen and highlights one part of the page at a time, with a short explanation of what it shows or does.

  • First visit: the guide starts automatically once when you visit a page for the first time in this browser, so you can quickly get oriented.
  • Replay any time: press the Guide button (compass icon) in the header whenever you want to walk through the current page again.
  • Controls: use Next and Back, or press → / ← on your keyboard. Press Done on the last step, click ×, or press Esc to close it.
  • Safe and read-only: the guide never changes data, never submits forms and never clicks buttons for you.

Common questions

The page says “Session expired”

Your sign-in lasts for a fixed number of hours. Click Sign in to sign in again; your work in progress (such as an unconfirmed scan draft) is kept on the server.

Windows sign-in window does not open

Your browser blocked the pop-up window. Allow pop-ups for this site and click Sign in with Windows (SSO) again, or sign in with your password.

Theme or language resets after closing the browser

Your browser may be clearing site storage or cookies (for example in a private window). Normal browser windows remember both settings.

登录与退出登录

受保护的模块页面需要使用你的 Dash 账号登录。根据服务器配置，支持以下三种方式：

  1. 从 Dash 应用进入：在 Dash 中点击对应模块按钮，会通过一次性链接自动登录并跳转。
  2. 账号和密码：打开登录页，输入工号、姓名或邮箱以及密码，点击登录。使用工号最不易重名。
  3. Windows 账号登录（SSO）：在登录页点击使用 Windows 账号登录（SSO）。在公司电脑上会弹出一个小窗口并在完成后自动关闭（请允许本站的弹出窗口）。

提示：点击右上角的退出按钮只会退出 dash_go 的登录状态，不会影响你在原 Python Dash 应用中的登录。

页面导览

页面导览会压暗背景并逐个高亮介绍页面上的主要区域，帮助你快速了解各部分的用途。

  • 首次访问自动引导：在当前浏览器首次访问某个页面时，导览会自动启动一次，帮助你快速熟悉界面。
  • 随时重看：点击顶栏中的导览按钮（指南针图标），可随时重新体验当前页面的完整导览。
  • 操作方式：点击下一步 / 上一步，或使用键盘方向键 → / ← 切换步骤；在最后一步点击完成、点击 × 或按 Esc 即可关闭。
  • 安全只读：导览不会修改任何数据、不会提交表单，也不会替你点击任何按钮。

常见问题

页面提示“登录已过期”

登录状态会在一定小时数后过期。点击登录重新登录即可；尚未确认的工作草稿（例如扫码草稿）仍保留在服务器上。

Windows 登录窗口没有弹出来

浏览器拦截了弹出窗口。请允许本站弹出窗口后再次点击使用 Windows 账号登录（SSO），或改用密码登录。

关闭浏览器后主题或语言恢复默认

浏览器可能开启了隐私模式或自动清除本地存储 / Cookie。正常窗口下会自动记住这两项设置。
